## What Is the GSEL for RMF ATO Accelerator?

The GSEL for RMF ATO Accelerator is a Governance Engineering reference implementation that demonstrates how NIST SP 800-37 Risk Management Framework authorization activities can be implemented as an engineered governance workflow — with CNSSI 1253 control tailoring, hash-chained evidence integrity, and a human authorization decision gate.

It is derived from the BridgeCore Governance Engineering Methodology™ (BGEM) and published through the Governance Systems Engineering Lab (GSEL) as a reference implementation demonstrating Governance Engineering in practice.

**Current Version:** v0.1.0
**Status:** Published Reference Implementation
**Formally Specified | Public Repository**

---

## The Problem It Solves

Organizations pursuing Authority to Operate (ATO) under the NIST RMF face two persistent challenges:

- Control selection and tailoring is manual, inconsistent, and difficult to audit
- Evidence generation is fragmented, leaving authorization packages vulnerable to integrity questions

The GSEL for RMF ATO Accelerator addresses both by engineering the authorization workflow — producing traceable, evidence-supported, and human-gated authorization processes rather than documentation alone.
